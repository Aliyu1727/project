I’m a Full Stack Developer based in Kano, Nigeria. My work brings together frontend and backend development, machine learning and problem-solving thoughtful collaboration, and a belief that the best solutions start with listening.

My path began with my National Diploma in Computer Science at Jigawa State Institute of Information Technology, Kazaure, and later furthered my studies in Bsc. Information and Communication Technology at Aliko Dangote University of Science and Technology, Wudil and has grown through building real web projects, learning modern frameworks, and exploring Machine Learning. I’m especially interested in Web Development and Machine Learning and work that creates useful, measurable value.

My goal is to keep learning, do work I can stand behind, and help good teams make meaningful progress.
